# ResQplan lifecycle audit

The current site already has a strong Calibrated Command Surface shell, role-aware login, persistent demo state, organization inputs, zone assignment, volunteer citizen collection, incident prioritization, resource assignment, responder status progression, and a shared Leaflet map.

The attached brief identifies these additions as the next scope: a post-disaster Recovery & Restoration module; recovery KPIs, prioritization explanations, task status transitions, and recovery markers; a pre-disaster Community Hazards map layer with category filters and marker detail; removal of Authority add-citizen controls while retaining volunteer collection and Authority read/search/filter access; a functional responder route modal from Rescue Station A to the assigned INC-1042 location with a Demo Route label and On the Way action; and navigation labels that clearly expose the full Prepare → Respond → Recover lifecycle.

Current gaps to address are localized to client/src/pages/Home.tsx and client/src/index.css. No backend, server, or database changes are needed for this simulated demonstration.
